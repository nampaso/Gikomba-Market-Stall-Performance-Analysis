## Business Scenario Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market, relying on daily handwritten notes for sales and manual inventory checks. To understand her business performance and guide growth, she needs a data-driven evaluation of her revenue patterns and stock levels. This project analyzes two weeks of daily sales data and stock inventory using core Python to assess week-on-week growth, compare weekday versus weekend performance, classify sales days, identify products requiring immediate restocking, and calculate product profit margins.

## Top Findings

1. **Revenue Growth:** Total sales increased from **KES 22,050.00** in Week 1 to **KES 24,400.00** in Week 2, reflecting a strong week-on-week growth rate of **10.66%**.
2. **Weekend Dominance:** Weekend sales averaged **KES 4,900.00** per day compared to **KES 2,825.00** on weekdays—representing a **73.45% increase** in daily revenue on weekends.
3. **Inventory & Margins:** **Tomatoes** (18 kg stock vs. 25 kg reorder level) and **Sukuma** (9 kg stock vs. 15 kg reorder level) require immediate reordering, while **Avocado** yields the highest gross margin at **42.86%**.
